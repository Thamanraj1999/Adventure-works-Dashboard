Adventure Works Sales Dashboard: Power BI, Power Query, DAX, Time Series Forecasting
Built a 4-page Power BI dashboard of Executive Dashboard, Map, Product detail and Customer detail using retail sales data (2020–2022) for Adventure Works.
 Cleaned and modeled data with Power Query; created DAX measures for sales, product, and customer analysis.
 Performed forecasting to estimate revenue and identify low-demand and high-growth products.
